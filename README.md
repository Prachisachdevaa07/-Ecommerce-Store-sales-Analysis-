# 📊 E-commerce Store Sales Data Analysis Using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, evaluate sales channel performance, and generate actionable business insights.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and data visualizations to track key performance indicators (KPIs) and present business performance in an easy-to-understand format.

This project was completed as part of my learning journey in **Data Analytics**, with the help of YouTube tutorials and hands-on practice.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales revenue and order volume.
- Compare purchasing behavior across male and female customers.
- Identify top-performing states based on sales revenue.
- Analyze order fulfillment outcomes, including delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders.
- Evaluate sales channel contribution across e-commerce platforms.
- Understand customer demographics using age groups and gender.
- Develop an interactive Excel dashboard for KPI monitoring.
- Generate business insights and recommendations from sales data.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains approximately **31,048 e-commerce transaction records** with information about orders, customers, products, sales, and shipping.

The dataset includes the following fields:

- Order ID and Customer ID
- Gender, Age, and Age Group
- Order Date and Month
- Order Status
- Sales Channel
- Product SKU, Category, Size, and Quantity
- Sales Amount and Currency
- Shipping City, State, Postal Code, and Country
- Business-to-Business (B2B) Indicator

*Note: The dataset figures and insights presented below are based on the analysis performed for this project.*

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following metrics:

- Total Sales Revenue
- Total Number of Orders
- Monthly Sales Performance
- Gender-wise Sales Revenue
- State-wise Sales Revenue
- Order Status Distribution
- Sales Channel Contribution
- Customer Demographic Analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The interactive Excel dashboard provides a consolidated view of e-commerce sales performance through:

- **Monthly Sales Analysis:** Tracks sales trends across different months.
- **Gender-wise Sales Analysis:** Compares sales contributions from male and female customers.
- **State-wise Sales Analysis:** Identifies the top five states by sales revenue.
- **Order Status Analysis:** Displays delivered, returned, cancelled, and refunded orders.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Compares performance across different e-commerce platforms.
- **Customer Demographic Analysis:** Examines sales patterns by age group and gender.
- **Interactive Visualizations:** Presents key metrics and trends through charts and Pivot Tables.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Overall Sales Performance

- Total sales revenue was approximately **₹2.12 crore**.
- The dataset contains approximately **31,048 orders**.
- March recorded the highest monthly sales revenue, at approximately ₹19.28 lakh.
- November recorded the lowest monthly sales revenue, at approximately ₹16.15 lakh.

### 2. Sales by Gender

- Female customers contributed approximately ₹1.36 crore in sales.
- Male customers contributed approximately ₹76.14 lakh in sales.
- Female customers accounted for approximately **64% of total sales revenue**.

These results indicate that female customers contributed a larger share of the recorded sales revenue.

### 3. Top-Performing States

The top five states by sales revenue were:

| Rank | State | Sales Revenue |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Maharashtra | ₹29.90 lakh |
| 2 | Karnataka | ₹26.46 lakh |
| 3 | Uttar Pradesh | ₹21.05 lakh |
| 4 | Telangana | ₹17.12 lakh |
| 5 | Tamil Nadu | ₹16.79 lakh |

**Key Insight:** Maharashtra generated the highest sales revenue among the states analyzed, followed by Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh.

### 4. Order Status Analysis

The recorded order outcomes were:

| Order Status | Number of Orders |
|---|---:|
| Delivered | 28,641 |
| Returned | 1,045 |
| Cancelled | 844 |
| Refunded | 517 |

**Key Insight:** Delivered orders represented the largest share of recorded order statuses. Returned, cancelled, and refunded orders can be monitored to identify opportunities for operational improvement.

### 5. Sales Channel Analysis

The leading sales channels by share of recorded orders were:

| Sales Channel | Share of Orders |
|---|---:|
| Amazon | 35.48% |
| Myntra | 23.36% |
| Flipkart | 21.59% |
| Ajio | 6.22% |
| Nalli | 4.78% |
| Meesho | 4.50% |
| Others | 4.06% |

**Key Insight:** Amazon was the leading sales channel, followed by Myntra and Flipkart. These platforms represented the majority of the recorded channel distribution.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can help improve business performance:

- **Improve Seasonal Planning:** Investigate monthly sales fluctuations and develop targeted campaigns during lower-performing months.
- **Strengthen Customer Engagement:** Use customer demographic insights to create relevant marketing campaigns and product recommendations.
- **Optimize Regional Strategies:** Focus on high-performing states while exploring growth opportunities in other regions.
- **Monitor Order Exceptions:** Track cancellations, returns, and refunds to identify recurring operational issues.
- **Optimize Sales Channels:** Evaluate channel-level performance to improve marketing allocation and sales efficiency.
- **Improve KPI Monitoring:** Maintain a consistent dashboard to monitor sales performance and support data-driven decisions.
